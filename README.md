# Totem Particles

![Totem Particles preview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Myowiri/TotemParticles/main/totemparticles.gif)

Give your Totem of Undying activations a fresh look with Totem Particles! Customize the particle effects to match your style, from subtle bursts to bold, colorful displays.

### 🎯 Features
- Turn off the vanilla totem pop animation
- Choose two custom particle colors
- Adjust particle scale and power
- Preview and fine-tune your preferred effect in-game

### ⚙️Settings
You can open the settings through Mod Menu or from Options → Accessibility Settings.

Whether you prefer a clean, minimal effect or a bright burst of color, Totem Particles lets you make every totem activation your own.
